    📖 How to play

    1. Goal: be the first to play all your tiles.
    2. Run = 3 or more of the same color in a row.
      456
      Group = 3 or 4 of the same number, all different colors.
      777
    3. First play: your sets must add up to 30 points or more, using only tiles from your rack.
    4. After that you can add to sets on the table — and even move table tiles around — as long as every set is right when you tap ✅ Done.
    5. Can't play? Tap 🎴 Draw to take a tile. That ends your turn.
    6. The joker ☺ can be any tile. To take a joker from the table, put the real tile it stands for in its place — then use the joker that same turn.

    👆 Drag tiles — or tap tiles, then tap where they go. Drop on the green table to start a new set. Use ↩️ Undo or ⟲ Reset if you get mixed up. 💡 Hint lights up tiles you could play.

    🏆 Scoring: when someone goes out, everyone else loses the points left in their rack (joker = 30) and the winner gets them.
